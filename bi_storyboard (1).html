Applied Business Intelligence — Storyboard

From Raw Data
to Real Decisions

Every business decision traces back to a data point somewhere. This storyboard walks through the eight stages that turn scattered, raw business data into a decision a manager actually acts on.

Running example: Each frame follows one thread — Café Bloom, a 40-outlet café chain — to show what each stage looks like in a real business, not just in theory.

SCENE 03 / 08 STAGE · STORAGE
Data Storage
Collected data needs a permanent, organized home. It's loaded into databases, data warehouses, or data lakes so it can be queried reliably instead of living in scattered files.
Café BloomAll incoming records land in a cloud data warehouse, organized by outlet, date, and product category.

SCENE 04 / 08 STAGE · PREPARATION
Data Processing (ETL)
Raw stored data is messy. It's cleaned, validated, and transformed — duplicates removed, errors fixed, formats standardized — so it becomes consistent and analysis-ready.
Café BloomDuplicate loyalty entries get merged, missing prices are filled in, and currency formats are standardized across all outlets.

SCENE 08 / 08 STAGE · DECISION
Managerial Decision & Action
The insight becomes a concrete decision — reallocating resources, changing strategy, adjusting operations. The action is implemented, its results are monitored, and outcomes are measured.
Café BloomStaffing and iced-beverage stock are increased at college-area outlets for the 2–5 PM window. Sales are tracked to measure the impact.
